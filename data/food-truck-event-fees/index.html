What food truck events charge vendors: 49 organizers' own applications

Every fee below is copied from the event organizer's own vendor page or application, with a link. Festivals, fairs, markets, concert series and truck rallies in the US, Canada, the UK and Australia. Read on 24 November 2026.

20%of sales, the median cut at the 21 events that take one
$150 a daymedian flat fee at 15 US events that charge a flat fee only
6 of 49events charge a flat fee and a cut of sales

16 of the 21 events that take a cut take more than 15%

The Washington State Food Truck Association tells event hosts that a cut of sales "should never exceed 15%", that a flat fee "should not exceed $300/day", and that vendors should be charged one or the other, "but NEVER both". Measured against that, the flat fees mostly pass: 3 of 15 are above $300 a day. The cuts mostly don't, and 6 events charge both.

The 5 state fairs in the sample all take a cut, from 15% to 25%, and none charges a flat fee only. The highest cut is 35%, at an arts festival that also takes a $1,500 non-refundable minimum.

How this was put together

We searched for food vendor applications and vendor pages published by the organizers themselves, opened every page or PDF, and copied the sentence that states the fee. Blog posts that only give typical ranges were left out, and so were pages that wouldn't load or didn't state a fee. Fees are as written, in the event's own currency, with no conversion. Four events come from one booking agent, and some organizers appear more than once for different events. Where a page shows next year's packet, the year is the one on the page.
