How long do wedding family photos take? What 39 photographers say on their own sites

Every number below is copied from a working wedding photographer's own website, with a link to the page. Read on 24 November 2026.

30 mintime blocked for family photos, median of 31 photographers
3.25 minper group photo, median of 20
12 groupsmost they suggest keeping the list to, median of 15

30 minutes fits about 9 groups, not 12

Put the medians together and they don't agree. At 3.25 minutes a group, the usual 30-minute slot holds about 9 group photos. The same photographers suggest lists of up to 12. A 12-group list at that pace needs about 39 minutes. If your list is longer than 9 groups, either ask for more time or cut groups.

Photographers in the UK tend to quote shorter slots, 10 to 30 minutes, and two of them say they are done in about 10. Some UK photographers count the wedding party in their "group shots", so the totals are not always measuring the same thing.

How this was put together

We searched for wedding photographers writing about family formals on their own websites, opened every page, and copied the sentence that holds the number. Magazines, listicles, forums and software companies were left out, and so were pages that no longer load. A range like "20 to 30 minutes" is kept as a range; medians use its midpoint. "Page dated" is the newest date the page shows, which is often when it was last updated rather than first written.

Three of the photographers (Liz Price, Taun Henderson, Nat & Bryan) use nearly the same wording for "3 minutes per group", which looks like a shared template. Each gives a different total, so all three are kept.
