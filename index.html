Four rules every tool on this bench follows

  1. No sign-up. Open the page and start. No account, no password.
  2. What you type stays on your device. It is saved in your own browser and isn't uploaded. Sharing works by a link that carries the data inside it.
  3. The main job is free. Pro is for people who use a tool every week. Every tool has a way to pay for it once; a yearly plan is only ever an extra option.
  4. It works on a phone. Every tool is checked at phone width before it goes up.

Made by Sam. Each tool starts from a job people still do on paper or in a spreadsheet, and complain about.
