SAMSBENCH

I'm Sam. I build small tools, one job each.

Every tool here started with people doing a job by hand: a family photo list scribbled on the back of a program, a food truck owner adding up booth fees on a calculator in the truck. When enough people complain about the same job online, and I can see some of them have already paid for a fix, it goes on the list.

How a tool gets picked

Before building anything I ask an AI chatbot the questions a real buyer would ask. If the chatbot can already do the whole job right there in the chat, the tool doesn't get built. You don't need a page for that.

How it gets built

One page, one job. The maths is checked against answers worked out by hand, and the page is checked at phone width before it goes up.

What happens to what you type

It is saved in your own browser on your device and isn't uploaded. When you share a plan, the data travels inside the link itself. The one thing that leaves your device is the email you enter to unlock Pro, so the purchase can be checked. I count page visits with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies.

How paying works

The main job of every tool is free. Pro is for people who use a tool every week, paid through Whop. Every tool has a way to pay for Pro once; Family Formals also has a yearly plan for busy photographers. You unlock it with the email you paid with. If something goes wrong, reply to your Whop receipt and I'll sort it out.

Some people who write or make videos for the same crowd recommend these tools and get half of each sale that comes through their link.
