Privacy

Short version: what you type into a tool stays in your browser. There are no accounts. A few small things do leave your device, and they are all listed here.

What stays on your device

Everything you type into a tool (names, lists, numbers, notes) is saved in your own browser on that device, so it is there next time. It is not uploaded to me. If you clear your browser data, or your browser clears it for you, it is gone. Some tools let you save a backup file; that file is yours.

When you share a plan with someone, the plan travels inside the link itself. Anyone you give the link to can see what is in it.

What leaves your device

Visit counts. I count page visits and button presses (for example "printed the cards") with GoatCounter. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't track you across sites. It records the page, the referring site, a rough location from your IP address, screen size and browser, and a short code from the link you came from (like ?via=…). It does not receive what you type into a tool.

Unlocking Pro. When you type the email you paid with, it is sent to a small checking service hosted on Whop, which asks Whop whether that email bought Pro for that tool and answers yes or no. When you type a gift code, a fingerprint of the code is sent to the same service. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is kept by me.

Paying. Payments happen on Whop. Whop handles your card and email under its own privacy policy; I see your email, what you bought and the amount, not your card.

Whop ad pixel (Family Formals only). The Family Formals page loads Whop's ad pixel, which tells Whop when someone views the page, starts using the planner or goes to checkout, so I can see whether Whop ads work. It is not loaded when the page is opened from a shared plan link, and it never receives the names in your list.

Fonts. The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for them.

Weather (Ship or Hold only). When you press Check, Ship or Hold asks the US National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) for the forecast at the place you entered.

Email

If you write to me, I keep the conversation to answer you. If I wrote to you about a tool and you reply "no", you won't hear from me again.
