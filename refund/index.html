Refunds

Every Pro purchase on samsbench can be refunded in full within 30 days. You don't need to give a reason.

How

Email sam@samsbench.com from the address you paid with, and say which tool. The refund goes back through Whop to the way you paid, usually within a few days of my reply. After a refund, that purchase no longer unlocks Pro.

Yearly plans

You can cancel a yearly plan at any time in your Whop account, so it won't renew. If a yearly charge went through and you didn't want it, email within 30 days of that charge for a full refund.

Gift codes

Gift codes are free, so there is nothing to refund.
